About this reconstruction

127 drawable models from 20 ZDT files, including their original polygon commands and palette entries from PAL3D.CAT. Empty placeholders are omitted. Articulated parts use their rest pose. The browser uses WebGL projection and depth buffering; it does not emulate the game's lighting, shading, or DOS renderer. Some shared palette colors are supplied by US3D.PAL. Each selection is scaled to fit the viewport. The original game files are unchanged.
